Common questions

QuestionAnswer
How do I aim and shoot?Drag anywhere to turn the gun, then tap to fire. Settings > Replay Tutorial shows the hand guide again.
I bought Remove Ads or the Starter Pack on another device.Open the Shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
I'm stuck on a level.Use boosters before the level starts, upgrade your weapon, or wait for the targets to line up before firing.
Can I play offline?Yes. Ads, leaderboards and the Daily Challenge reward need a connection; the levels don't.
How do I turn off reminders or change the language?Both are in the game's Settings.
Will my progress move to a new iPhone?Progress is saved on the device. It moves with an iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer.
