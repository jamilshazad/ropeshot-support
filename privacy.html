RopeShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

RopeShot is a single-player shooting puzzle game for iPhone. It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. This policy explains the small amount of data the game and its partners handle, and the choices you have.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades, settings and streaks) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it, and Settings > Reset Progress clears it in the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

RopeShot is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can watch for a reward, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, device model, iOS version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and crash or performance data from the ads software.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a US state with privacy laws, the game shows Google's consent form the first time it opens, and you can choose how your data is used for ads. Separately, iOS asks whether RopeShot may track you; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying "Remove Ads" in the shop turns off the between-level ads.

Gameplay statistics

To see where players get stuck, the game may send anonymous gameplay events, such as which level was started, cleared or failed, to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These events carry a random ID created on your device (stored as a one-way hash), never your advertising identifier, name or contact details, and they are not used for ads. See the TelemetryDeck privacy policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, never your payment details. Restore Purchases in the shop or Settings brings back what you bought.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Endless and Daily Challenge scores are sent to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Daily-challenge reminders are optional, scheduled on your device, and contain no ads. Turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

RopeShot is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to show, change or delete. For data held by Google for advertising, use Google's consent form in the game (Settings > Privacy Choices, where shown) or My Ad Center. You can also contact us with any request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
